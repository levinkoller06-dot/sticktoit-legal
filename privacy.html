Privacy Policy

Stick To It Uploader

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Overview

Stick To It Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the team behind the TikTok channel Stick To It (@sticktoitdaily). It publishes our own videos through the TikTok Content Posting API.

We do not collect, process, or store personal data of any other person.

2. What the app stores

The app stores one thing locally on the machine where it runs:

  • the access token that lets it upload to the Stick To It account.

This token is kept in a local file on that machine and is never sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API. It is deleted when it is no longer needed.

No video files, captions, analytics, or account data are stored beyond the moment they are sent to TikTok.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from viewers or from any other TikTok user.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer data to third parties.
  • It does not store data on external servers.

4. TikTok

Data sent to TikTok through the API is handled under TikTok's own privacy policy and terms. The app itself keeps no copy.

5. Retention

The only stored item, the access token, is removed once the app no longer needs it. Nothing else is retained.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the TikTok channel Stick To It (@sticktoitdaily). No other contact channel is offered.
